Day · light

The Vault Times

Vol. I  ·  Broadsheet Edition  ·  Monday, June 1

Obsidian has never read quite like newsprint before. Broadsheet sets your notes in a high-contrast editorial type system — a Didone headline face over a screen-tuned serif — and lets a single electric accent punch through the black and white.

The result is a workspace that feels like the front page of a well-made paper, yet keeps the modern spacing and color you expect from a contemporary app. Links, markers and kickers all carry the accent so the eye always knows where to travel next.

Setting the Headline

Section heads sit beneath crisp rules. Smaller heads become small-caps kickers that label a passage the way a newspaper labels its columns.

Field Notes

  • Drop cap opens the first paragraph
  • Pull-quotes lean into the accent rule
  • Tables are ruled like a stats box
“The detail that makes it feel like print is the rule — a hairline of ink that organizes everything below it.”
